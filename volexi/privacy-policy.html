1. Introduction

Welcome to Volexi ("Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We strongly believe in fundamental privacy rights and are committed to protecting the privacy of our players.

Volexi is an elegant, fast-paced word puzzle game designed for solo play. We do not track you, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect personal identification information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using Volexi on iOS and iPadOS.

By downloading and playing Volexi, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Data Collection and Processing

We do NOT collect, harvest, or store any personal data on external private servers.

Volexi operates locally on your device and relies strictly on Apple's native, secure system services:

A. Apple Game Center (Leaderboards & Authentication)

  • Volexi integrates Apple GameKit for leaderboards and score tracking.
  • The game uses silent authentication with Apple Game Center. We only receive your Game Center player alias and your high score rankings directly through Apple's official GameKit API to display your global standing.
  • We do not store your Game Center login credentials or personal profile. All Game Center interactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy .

A. Apple iCloud Sync (CloudKit / Key-Value Storage)

  • Your in-game energy balance and high scores are synchronized across your devices via Apple's native NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore (iCloud).
  • This synchronization occurs encrypted inside your private Apple iCloud container. We have no access to your iCloud account, Apple ID, files, or personal information.

C. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

  • Any energy booster purchases are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit.
  • We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card, bank details, or billing address. Apple handles all transactions, receipt validation, and tax compliance directly under Apple's terms.

D. Local Device Storage

  • Preferences such as your selected game theme, sound settings, language preference, and local game statistics are stored strictly on your local device storage (UserDefaults).

E. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

  • Volexi contains no third-party SDKs (such as Google Firebase, Facebook SDK, Adjust, or RevenueCat).
  • Volexi contains no advertisements and performs no cross-app tracking.

3. App Privacy Nutrition Label (App Store)

Under Apple's App Privacy Nutrition Label guidelines, Volexi qualifies as:

  • Data Used to Track You: None (0).
  • Data Linked to You: None (0).
  • Data Not Linked to You: Game Center scores and achievements managed directly by Apple.

4. Children's Privacy

Volexi does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app complies fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K guidelines.

5. Data Security and Retention

Because we do not operate external databases or backend servers holding your personal data, there is no risk of your personal identity being compromised from our systems. Your local game data remains on your personal device and inside your private Apple iCloud account, which you can delete or reset at any time in the game's Settings menu.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy frameworks (including GDPR Articles 15–22 and CCPA):

  • You have full control over your game data.
  • You can reset your statistics and local scores at any time from within the Settings > Reset Stats screen.
  • You can manage or disable Game Center sharing and iCloud data sync directly in your iOS/iPadOS Device Settings > Apple ID.
